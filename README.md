# Daniel Vofchuk

**Chief Technology Officer @ Cainvest Group** · Fintech & Digital Assets
São Paulo, Brazil

Full-stack engineer and technology executive with 8+ years across the complete product
lifecycle. I lead engineering for crypto / digital-asset and banking products — custody,
wallets, on-chain settlement, core ledger, payments and KYC/AML compliance — with a focus
on security, reliability and compliance. Founder of [Vizo](https://vizo.dev), a software
studio delivering products for clients in Brazil, the UK and Israel.
